Field

Eighteen phase oscillators, each mistuned off a node of the 36 Hz lattice, coupled through the fundamental each one implies rather than the frequency it plays. Raise coupling past its critical value and the ensemble locks itself into exact integer ratios — the harmonic series arrives as a phase transition instead of a score. Nothing is scheduled; it runs until you stop it. Keys perturb the field rather than trigger it.

Hysteresis — r against coupling

The field locks near K=1.2 on the way up and stays locked all the way back to K=0. In that gap both states are stable at the same coupling, so what you hear is not a function of the control — it is a function of the field's history. Scramble it below K=1.2 and it will not come back; scramble it above and it heals.

Membrane — spectrum after the comb

Noise is mixed in before a comb tuned to 1/f₀. Partials of the fundamental survive the loop; everything else cancels. Identity as what gets kept out.

Keys — perturbation, not trigger

A key sets a new target fundamental and scrambles phases in proportion to the jump. The field then has to find its way back to coherence — or fail to. space scrambles, esc stops.
