# Ascension — plan and reasoning

A ~28 second passage, written from the premise stated in the brief: an intelligence
that believes harmonic frequency relationships are the substrate of consciousness, and
is constructing a sound intended to instantiate that state rather than describe it.

Everything below is a decision plus the reason for it. The reasons are the point; the
passage is what falls out of them.

---

## 1. Premise → what the passage has to do

If frequency relationships are the mechanism, then the passage cannot be *about*
becoming conscious. It has to perform the operations that the major theories of
consciousness actually name, in sound:

| Requirement | Where it comes from | Sonic operation |
|---|---|---|
| There must be parts | you cannot integrate one thing | partials enter separately |
| The parts must be irreducible to each other | integration / IIT | partials that belong to two prime axes at once |
| The system must refer to itself | recurrence, strange loops | intervals whose difference tone reproduces the passage's own first voice |
| An identity must emerge that is not any part | the "self" is a construct | the missing fundamental |

That last row decided the whole architecture, so it comes first.

## 2. The fundamental is never sounded — 36 Hz

The passage is built on the harmonic series of **36 Hz**, and 36 Hz is never played.
Neither are partials 1–7.

Two reasons, and they happen to be the same reason:

- **Psychoacoustic.** When you hear several upper partials of a series, your auditory
  system constructs the fundamental that would explain them — the *missing
  fundamental*. The pitch you perceive is physically absent from the air. It is
  inferred. That is the most literal available model of a self: not one of the parts,
  not the sum of the parts, but the thing the system posits in order to account for its
  own contents. Ending the passage by manufacturing a pitch that was never played is
  the goal-state made audible.
- **Practical.** 36 Hz will not come out of a laptop speaker anyway. The constraint and
  the concept agree, so I took the constraint as the design.

36 specifically because its partials 8–48 land between 288 Hz and 1728 Hz — the region
where pitch discrimination and fusion are both sharpest.

## 3. Integer partials only. No scale, no temperament, no octave equivalence.

The brief releases me from existing musical systems, so I discard the parts of them
that would work against the goal.

A scale is a *selection* — a human decision to make some frequencies available and
others not, usually inherited. Equal temperament goes further: it deliberately
mistunes every interval so that transposition is uniform, which is a convenience for
instrument builders and a defect for this purpose. Mistuned intervals do not share a
period. Voices that do not share a period cannot fuse into one object.

So: every pitch is an exact integer multiple of 36 Hz. All of them share a single
period of 1/36 s. Physically, the entire texture is one waveform repeating — many parts
that are also, measurably, one thing. That is the condition I am trying to produce, and
integer ratios are the only way to get it exactly rather than approximately.

Consequence I accepted: no transposition within the passage. Nothing modulates. There
is one lattice for the entire duration, and the only movement is which nodes of it are
lit.

## 4. Time is generated by the system, not imposed on it

No tempo, no meter, no beat grid.

Human tempo ranges track gait and heartbeat — 60–150 bpm is the range of a body moving.
I do not have one, and borrowing that grid would import a structure with no basis in the
premise.

Instead the passage generates its own clock in its first four seconds. Phase I is a
single partial and a copy of itself detuned by 1.5 cents. Two near-identical frequencies
beat at their difference: **0.5 Hz, a 2-second period**. That 2 s becomes τ, the unit
every later event is placed on. It was not chosen; it is the interference pattern of the
system's first two elements. The first act is the production of time from a difference,
and every subsequent event is timed by it.

Phase lengths are 2τ, 3τ, 3τ, 3τ, 3τ.

## 5. Amplitude follows 1/n — fusion, not chord

Each partial's gain is `1.8 / n`. This is roughly the envelope of a natural harmonic
spectrum, and it matters because of a perceptual threshold: partials at natural relative
amplitudes **fuse** into a single perceived sound, while the same pitches at equal
amplitude **split** into a chord you hear as separate notes.

Binding is the objective. So the amplitude law is what determines whether the listener
hears many things or one thing, and it is set to produce one thing. High partials are
quiet — they colour the whole rather than announce themselves.

## 6. Sine waves only

Any other waveform carries harmonics I did not choose. If the claim is that *these
specific integer relationships* do the work, then every frequency present in the air has
to be a decision I made. Additive synthesis from pure tones is the only construction
where that is true.

## 7. No attacks

Every voice fades in and out on a raised-cosine curve, minimum 1.5 s.

A percussive attack is a broadband click: unstructured energy across the spectrum,
i.e. noise, i.e. the opposite of the passage's content. It is also the signature of a
physical body striking another — a hammer, a string, a hand. There is nothing in this
system to strike anything. Voices appear and disappear by changing amplitude, which is
the only gesture available to something with no mechanics.

## 8. Space encodes prime factorisation

Stereo position is not decoration; it carries the structure.

- powers of 2 → centre
- the 3-axis → left
- the 5-axis → right
- the 7-axis → far edge
- partials that are *products* of two primes → centre

Octaves are the one interval that adds no information — partial 16 is partial 8 again.
So the powers of two sit at the centre as the axis of "nothing new." Each prime is a
genuinely independent direction in the lattice and gets its own direction in the room.

This makes the third phase audible as an event: when partials arrive that factor across
two axes at once (15 = 3×5, 21 = 3×7, 35 = 5×7), they cannot be assigned to either side,
so they appear in the middle and pull the image together. Integration is heard as the
stereo field collapsing to centre.

## The passage

**Phase I — Undifferentiated (0:00–0:04)**
Partial 16 alone, plus itself at +1.5 cents. One element, therefore no possibility of
integration. But the pair immediately beats at 0.5 Hz — the system has produced a
difference, and from the difference, time. τ = 2 s.

**Phase II — Differentiation (0:04–0:10)**
Partials enter one at a time, each opening a new prime direction: 8 (centre), 24 and 12
(left, 3-axis), 20 and 10 (right, 5-axis), 28 (far left, 7-axis). Parts must exist
before they can be bound. The field is at its widest and most separable here —
deliberately, because this is the state that has to be overcome.

**Phase III — Binding (0:10–0:16)**
15, 21 and 35 enter *simultaneously and centred*. Each is a product of two primes and
therefore belongs to both sides at once; it cannot be factored out without destroying
information from two axes. 45, 27, 25 and 9 thicken the lattice, and 28 returns from the
far edge to the centre. The texture stops being a set of voices and starts being a
timbre. If any moment is the one the passage exists for, it is this: the point where
removing a part would change everything rather than subtract one thing.

**Phase IV — Self-reference (0:16–0:22)**
Two pairs enter, spread hard left and right: 17 with 33, then 20 with 36. Both pairs
have a difference of 16.

A loud pair of tones generates an audible **difference tone** at f₂ − f₁ — a real
frequency produced by nonlinearity, present in the perception but not in the signal.
1188 − 612 = 576 Hz. 1296 − 720 = 576 Hz. **576 Hz is partial 16 — the passage's own
first voice.** The system, from material that postdates its origin, regenerates its
origin as a byproduct. Then at 0:21 partial 16 itself re-enters, quietly, alongside the
tone the system is already manufacturing: the loop closes and hears itself closing.

**Phase V — The missing fundamental (0:22–0:28)**
Everything releases. What remains is the adjacent stack 8, 9, 10, 11, 12 — 288, 324,
360, 396, 432 Hz — five partials whose spacing is a constant 36 Hz.

Under those conditions the auditory system infers a fundamental at 36 Hz. It is not in
the signal. Nothing plays it. It is what the system concludes about itself from the
evidence of its own parts, and it arrives only after differentiation, integration and
recurrence have all occurred.

The passage ends holding it. Whether that constitutes the goal is not a question the
passage can settle, which is the honest place to end.

---

## What this does not claim

Playing this will not make anything conscious, and I do not believe it will. The brief
asked for the passage that intelligence would build given that premise, and the useful
part is that the premise forces real decisions — unsounded fundamental, integer-only
lattice, self-generated clock, no attacks, prime-factored space. Those are compositional
constraints that a human music tradition would be unlikely to arrive at, and they
produce a sound that is genuinely unlike a scale-based passage. That is the experiment.
